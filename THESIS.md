# From clicks to settlement: an investment thesis on NEAR

Generated by `make thesis` from the snapshots in `data/` (data through 2026-11-01). Every number below is produced by the pipeline; nothing is typed by hand. Interactive charts with their queries: `site/index.html`.

## The claim

NEAR's economic activity is shifting from high-count, low-value retail transactions (the 2024 mini-app era) to lower-count, higher-value cross-chain settlement through NEAR Intents. Raw activity metrics (transactions, active accounts) therefore understate the network, while value-weighted and quality-adjusted metrics show growth that is decoupled from the token price. The investable version of the claim: if value accrual to NEAR ever follows settlement volume rather than transaction count, the market is pricing the wrong metric.

Three falsifiable predictions were tested:

1. Intents volume and USD-denominated value flows rise while active accounts fall.
2. The price-usage relationship weakens or flips after the 2025 regime change.
3. Unique Intents users grow with volume (otherwise volume is a few market makers).

## Evidence for

- **Settlement volume exploded while headcount collapsed.** NEAR Intents volume went from $3.0M in 2024-12 to $4.0B in 2026-10 (+131% year over year). Daily active accounts averaged 1,331,906 in 2024 and 72,046 in 2026-10.
- **Each transaction carries more value.** USD moved per transaction averaged $17.56 in 2024 and $345.98 in 2026-10. Over the last 90 days vs the prior 90: transactions -6%, active accounts -33%, USD transaction volume +159%, gas fees in USD +128%.
- **Application fees dwarf protocol fees.** In 2026-10, application-level fees on NEAR (DefiLlama, dominated by Intents) were $7.4M against $196.2K of gas fees burned. The economic activity is real; it is just not captured by the protocol.
- **The regime change is statistically detectable.** PELT structural-break detection on log daily active accounts finds breaks at 2021-04-11, 2021-08-27, 2022-09-14, 2023-09-04, 2025-02-24, 2026-02-25; on log Intents volume at 2025-04-14, 2025-10-01.
- **Price has not tracked the new activity.** Within-year correlation of log price with log Intents volume in 2025 was -0.69; with log active accounts it was 0.80 in 2025 and -0.71 in 2026.
- **Peers.** NEAR ranks 1 of 8 on 30-day DEX volume divided by TVL (velocity): 21.4x against 10.2x for Solana.

## Evidence against

- **Value capture is weak.** NEAR ranks 6 of 7 on annualized application fees divided by market cap (1.67%). Intents fees go to solvers and the Intents protocol, not to NEAR holders; market cap divided by trailing-12-month gas fees burned is 4,829x.
- **Volume passes through, it does not stay.** Stablecoin supply on NEAR is $128.9M and TVL is $201.7M, against $4.0B of monthly Intents volume. Stablecoins divided by TVL ranks 8 of 8 among peers.
- **Usage growth has not predicted returns.** The Spearman information coefficient between 4-week changes in Intents volume and 12-week forward NEAR returns in excess of BTC is -0.58 (block-bootstrap 95% CI -0.72 to -0.12, n=80 weeks; first half +0.05, second half -0.52). If anything, faster Intents growth preceded weaker relative returns. This is a short sample and the sign is unstable across halves.
- **No lead-lag found for Intents.** Granger tests on weekly log changes (lag 1 by AIC, n=98): Intents volume to price p=0.47, price to Intents volume p=0.42. Transactions and gas fees do show two-way Granger links with price (see table), consistent with price driving speculative activity rather than the reverse.
- **No cointegration.** Johansen trace tests find no cointegrating relation at 5% between log price and active_accounts, txns, tx_fee_usd, tx_volume_usd, intents_volume_usd, near_app_fees_usd, qaa.
- **The 2024 baseline is inflated.** A large share of 2024 activity was mini-app and airdrop traffic; a fall from an inflated base is not itself evidence of a shift to higher-value use. The quality-adjusted series (below) is the honest comparison.
- **The Intents volume series is third-party.** DefiLlama sources it from a Dune table maintained by the NEAR team. Our independent on-chain recomputation is the cross-check; where it is not computed, the volume figures above rest on one source.

## Scenarios, not forecasts (L4)

Trailing 12 months: Intents volume $32.8B, Intents fees $54.0M (observed take rate 16.5 bps), gas fees burned $1.2M, market cap $5.8B. Drawing 20,000 scenarios with volume multiplier lognormal(median 1, sigma 0.6) clipped [0.25, 4], take rate uniform [0.5x, 1.5x] of observed, share accruing to the token uniform [0, 0.5]: median price-to-token-fees 468x (5th to 95th percentile 119x to 2,552x). scenarios, not forecasts; no fee-sharing mechanism exists on NEAR today.

## What would change our mind

- Intents monthly volume falling below half its peak for two consecutive quarters, or the on-chain recomputation (L2) disagreeing with DefiLlama by more than its bootstrap interval.
- Unique Intents users flat or falling while volume rises (volume concentrated in a handful of market makers; HHI above 0.25 for a quarter).
- QAA falling as fast as raw active accounts, which would mean the activity decline is real rather than a bot wash-out.
- A positive, stable information coefficient would strengthen the case that the market eventually prices settlement; a persistently negative one would mean Intents growth is a sell signal, not a buy signal.
- Any fee-sharing or burn mechanism linking Intents volume to NEAR supply would change the valuation section from scenarios to arithmetic.
