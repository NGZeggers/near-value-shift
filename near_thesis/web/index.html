From clicks to settlement

A reproducible investment thesis on NEAR: is activity shifting from many low-value transactions to fewer, high-value cross-chain settlements through NEAR Intents, and does that show up in anything but the price? Every chart lists its source and query. The full generated argument is THESIS.md; the executed notebook is walkthrough.html. Every number on this page is computed by make build from committed snapshots.

Built for the NEAR Hackathon 2026, track Investment Thesis. MIT licensed. Sources: NearBlocks, DefiLlama, FastNear block extracts, NEAR RPC, CoinGecko and GitHub. BigQuery is an optional extraction path.
