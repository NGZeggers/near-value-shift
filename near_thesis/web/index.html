A reproducible investment thesis on NEAR

From clicks
to settlement

Is activity shifting from many low-value transactions to fewer, high-value cross-chain settlements through NEAR Intents, and does that show up in anything but the price? Every number on this page is computed by make build from committed snapshots, and every chart lists its source and query.

Reproduce it

For judges and anyone who wants to check the numbers rather than trust them.

  1. uv sync --locked --all-extrasinstalls the locked dependencies; no wallet, no Google account.
  2. make buildrebuilds every table, model, chart, THESIS.md and this page from committed inputs, offline.
  3. make testruns the regression suite, including the pricing, signing-control and receipt-status tests.
  4. make check-generatedconfirms the artifacts you just built match the committed ones byte for byte or within numeric tolerance.
