From clicks to settlement

A reproducible investment thesis on NEAR: is activity shifting from many low-value transactions to fewer, high-value cross-chain settlements through NEAR Intents, and does that show up in anything but the price? Every chart lists its source and query. Read the argument in THESIS.md.

Built for the NEAR Hackathon 2026, track Investment Thesis. MIT licensed. Sources: NearBlocks, DefiLlama, NEAR RPC, CoinGecko, GitHub, Google BigQuery public dataset crypto_near_mainnet_us.
