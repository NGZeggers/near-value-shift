# Reconciliation of the on-chain Intents estimate (generated)

Generated by `make thesis` from `data/derived/models/intents_reconciliation.json`; numbers are never typed by hand. The method, the declared tolerance and the holdout rule live in `near_thesis/reconcile.py`.

## Economic unit of comparison

- **unit:** USD notional of settled swaps per UTC calendar day on intents.near
- **our measure:** half gross participant notional: for each successful execute_intents receipt, for each (account, intent_hash) take the larger of priced inflow and priced outflow, sum over participants and divide by two. One two-party swap of 100 USDC against NEAR counts 100 USD once.
- **scope:** token_diff events emitted by intents.near in receipts with an explicit successful execution status; all chains and assets that appear in those events; no deposits, withdrawals or transfers
- **day boundary:** UTC; a sampled window is attributed to the planned UTC day
- **prices:** DefiLlama coins daily USD prices (CoinGecko ids from the committed 1Click registry), stablecoins at par, at most seven-day forward fill; unknown or unpriced assets contribute zero
- **reference:** DefiLlama dexs/near-intents dailyVolume = SUM(volume_amount_usd) FROM dune.near.dataset_near_intents_metrics per date_at (adapter source: DefiLlama/dimension-adapters dexs/near-intents/index.ts). The dataset is a curated Dune table maintained for NEAR's metrics; whether volume_amount_usd is one-sided or two-sided notional is not published, so the two measures are compared, not assumed identical.
- **shared origin:** DefiLlama does not compute this series itself; it queries Dune. Every public reference found derives from NEAR's own metrics datasets (Dune dune.near.*, the ClickHouse table behind near-intents.org, revenue.near.org volumeUsd), so none is of independent origin; the Dune series also covers confidential intents, which emit no public token_diff logs and are therefore outside what any on-chain log extraction can observe (see reference_definition).

**Pooled result.** Estimate / reference 0.253, conditional day-bootstrap interval [0.178, 0.327] over 46 matched days. The per-day table (`intents_reconciliation_daily.csv`) lists observed sampled volume, expansion factor, expanded estimate, reference, difference, window split, unpriced legs and unvalued account-intents for each of the 49 sampled days.

Confidential intents do not emit public token_diff logs, so they are structurally outside our on-chain measure while inside the reference. ## How to extend the evidence

1. `make blocks-intraday` fetches 24 hourly windows for the three motivating days and `make blocks-intraday-validation` the three validation days chosen by position (several hours each under the public rate limit); `make build` recomputes the factor and its validation.
2. `make blocks BLOCKS=100` doubles the blocks per fixed window; the per-day table and the holdout are recomputed by `make build`.
3. A complete-day extract needs BigQuery (`make bq`, credentials) or an archival node; the reconciliation code accepts any extract with the same schema.
4. A second reference independent of Dune needs an Intents Explorer JWT or a Dune API key; neither is available to this team.
